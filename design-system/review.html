Design rules — review list

Every published page, after the four rules went on.

  • sub-text is Myriad light, not regular
  • every text button is 48px tall with the same padding
  • hover fills brand blue #41647b and drops the outline — never black
  • a white button over a photograph or video is a 28% veil with white type
  • inside the blue CTA band the hover goes a step darker, or it would vanish

“open” is your local server, “live” is the page as published now — open both to compare. Ticks are remembered in this browser.
