The sale plan

From first valuation to final handover.

The original Jeff Brown framework of Prep, Listing, Cultivate, and Sold is expanded into a more confidence-building path with clearer deliverables and better conversion moments.

Private market valuation

Before a listing agreement, the broker frames the likely value range, buyer demand, time-on-market expectations, and the first tactical decision: price for speed, net return, or a controlled market test.

  • Comparable sold boats and currently listed alternatives.
  • Condition, upgrades, options, maintenance history, and location review.
  • Recommended asking price range with rationale and next steps.

Preparation that protects value

Small issues become negotiation leverage when buyers find them first. The prep stage creates a cleaner story before photography, showings, survey, and sea trial.

  • Staging, detailing, service recommendations, and document collection.
  • Surveyor and marine service referrals when additional confidence is needed.
  • Seller checklist for equipment, title, maintenance, and recent work.

Listing launch

The yacht goes live with complete media, searchable copy, and coordinated distribution instead of being slowly assembled after buyers have already formed a first impression.

  • Photography, drone/video, 360 tour planning, and listing description.
  • Jeff Brown website feature plus YachtWorld, MLS feeds, Boat Trader, and partner channels.
  • Broker-to-broker introduction for qualified buyer matching.

Lead cultivation

Inquiry volume matters less than quality. The team qualifies buyers, coordinates private and virtual showings, follows up fast, and translates buyer feedback into decisions.

  • CRM follow-up, buyer qualification, and showing coordination.
  • Weekly reporting on inquiries, listing views, feedback, and next actions.
  • Adjustments to price, presentation, or distribution when the market signals it.

Offer, survey, and sea trial

Once a qualified buyer is serious, the broker manages negotiation, contingencies, survey logistics, sea trial expectations, and any repair-credit discussion.

  • Offer strategy, counteroffers, deposit milestones, and contract guidance.
  • Survey and sea trial coordination with clear seller preparation.
  • Negotiation support after findings, without unnecessary emotion in the room.

Closing and handover

The final stage is where details matter most. Documentation, taxes, duties, transport, delivery, and handover are coordinated so the sale ends cleanly.

  • Closing documentation and coordination with required parties.
  • Transport, delivery, and post-sale handover planning.
  • Support for the seller’s next purchase, trade, service, or management need.
