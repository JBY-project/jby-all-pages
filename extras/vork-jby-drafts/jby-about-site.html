Company

Built for yacht buyers, sellers, and owners who expect a more personal experience.

Jeff Brown Yachts is a luxury yacht dealership and brokerage company headquartered in San Diego with offices across key boating markets in California, the Pacific Northwest, Hawaii, and the East Coast. The company represents a carefully selected portfolio of premium yacht brands while also supporting clients through brokerage, service, and ownership guidance.

What makes JBY different is the way the business combines boutique attention with a wider office network. Clients work with people who understand their local water, their boating goals, and the practical details that come after the sale, from private showings and trade-in conversations to service coordination and long-term support.
