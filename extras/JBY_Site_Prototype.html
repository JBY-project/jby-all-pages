Tue: 12:00 AM – 05:00 PM
View More

Vessels on Display

Description

The annual Oceanic Boat Show brings the industry together for a weekend on the water. This year features a wide range of vessels, from motor yachts to sailing boats, all equipped with current technology. The event takes place at Marina Bay, a fitting backdrop for owners and industry professionals alike.

Attendees can explore the latest in marine design and technology through interactive exhibits and live demonstrations. Builders and designers on site share where boating is headed next. Whether you are a seasoned owner or new to the water, there is something worth seeing.

In addition to the impressive lineup of boats, the Oceanic Boat Show will feature a series of seminars and workshops led by experts in the field. Topics will range from sustainable boating practices to investment opportunities in the marine industry. Don't miss the chance to network with fellow boating aficionados and discover new ventures.

In the evening, private events take place aboard a selection of the yachts on display, with food, wine, and the chance to meet industry leaders and fellow owners. It is more than a boat show. It is a gathering of the people who care most about being on the water.

Please note: RSVP-ing reserves a time slot to view the yacht at the show, but it does not include entry to the show itself. For ticket information, please visit the official show organizer's website.

Headquartered in San Diego, Jeff Brown Yachts has set the benchmark for excellence in yacht sales and service across the US coasts.

Our Story

After a 25-year career building one of the most successful yacht dealerships in the western United States, Jeff and Karen Brown changed course and opened a bespoke brokerage on a smaller scale for a boutique experience.

That smaller operation grew into something larger than planned. Today JBY represents an elite lineup of brands, including Riva, Wally, Pershing, Sirena, Brabus, Axopar, Jeanneau, and Four Winns, across seven home ports, while keeping the personal, one-on-one approach it was founded on.

Every client works directly with a licensed, bonded agent. Every yacht is supported by our own service and warranty department. That's the benchmark.
