Brokerage FAQ

We offer boutique, personalized service from a licensed and bonded brokerage team, built on Jeff Brown’s three decades in the marine industry. Every sales program is tailored to the vessel and the seller, never a one-size-fits-all script.

From motor and sailing yachts to family cruisers, expedition yachts, one-design boats, and custom vessels, we build a specific strategy for each one, regardless of size or class.

We guide you through a clear four-step process, Prep, Listing, Cultivate, Sold, from valuation and staging through marketing, negotiation, and handover.

With eight offices, San Diego, Newport Beach, Marina del Rey, Sausalito, Seattle, Kailua-Kona, Wrightsville Beach, and Charleston, we offer local expertise with reach across every major boating market we serve.

Your listing starts with professional photography, drone footage, and 360-degree tours, then goes out through MLS and international listings, our website, and targeted advertising.

We handle buyer inquiries and follow-up, private and virtual showings, offer facilitation, contract management, surveys, taxes, duties, transport arrangements, and the final handover.

You’ll work with licensed brokers and bonded agents, each bringing years of hands-on sales experience to every transaction.
