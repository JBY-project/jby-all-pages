Fort Lauderdale International Boat Show

A standout showing for Jeff Brown Yachts at the world's largest in-water boat show, with a curated fleet and a full week of viewings.

Oct 30 – Nov 3, 2024Fort Lauderdale, FLBoat Show

About the event

Jeff Brown Yachts returned to the Fort Lauderdale International Boat Show with one of our strongest line-ups yet. Across the week, our team welcomed owners, brokers, and first-time buyers aboard a curated selection of the brands we represent, from bold day boats to serene long-range cruisers.

Guests stepped aboard for private walkthroughs, met our sales professionals from offices across the country, and got a first look at several models making their U.S. debut. It was a week of great conversations, new friendships, and more than a few handshakes on the dock.

Thank you to everyone who stopped by our display. If you missed us in Fort Lauderdale, our team is always happy to arrange a private viewing or sea trial near you.
