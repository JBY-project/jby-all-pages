2.Selling a yacht

We look at recent sales of comparable yachts, what is currently on the market, the specification and refit history of your yacht and how it presents in person. You get a valuation range with the reasoning behind it, not just a number.

Send the details through the valuation form on our Sell Your Yacht page and a broker will come back to you.

Photography and video of the yacht, a full listing page with specification and deck plans, distribution to the brokerage networks, our own audience and newsletter, presence at the boat shows we attend, and one broker who owns the sale from first enquiry to closing.

You also get straight reporting: what enquiries came in, who viewed the yacht and what the market said about the price.

It depends on the model, the asking price and the season, so anyone who promises you a date is guessing. What we can promise is honest feedback: if the market is telling us the price is wrong, you will hear it early rather than after a quiet six months.

The running of the yacht, so that ownership feels like use rather than work: maintenance planned across the year, yard periods booked and supervised, crew support, vendor and invoice management, insurance and documentation dates tracked, and reporting you can read in a couple of minutes.

Our own marina and boatyard in San Diego handles service, repair and refit work, and our service team coordinates yard periods at other locations along the coast when that is closer to where you keep the yacht.
